Product Manual Excerpt — Model X200 Wireless Headphones

System requirements: Bluetooth 5.0 or later, companion app requires
iOS 15+ or Android 11+.

Setup instructions: press and hold the power button for 3 seconds
until the LED flashes blue, then open the companion app and select
"Add Device" to complete pairing.

Battery life is rated at 8 hours per charge under typical use.
