Contract Dispute Summary — Case #6120

Plaintiff alleges the vendor failed to deliver the agreed quarterly
maintenance visits under section 3.1 of the service agreement, missing
two of four scheduled visits in Q2 2026. Vendor states one visit was
rescheduled at the client's request and the other was delayed due to a
documented weather closure, both noted in the shared calendar log.

Both parties have submitted the relevant calendar exports as exhibits.
